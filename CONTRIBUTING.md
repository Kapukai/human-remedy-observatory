# Contributing

This is an exploratory, source-linked research atlas. Corrections are welcome.

For each proposed change, supply the family ID, source URL, court and date, pinpoint page or paragraph, whether the statement is an allegation or finding, proposed field value, and the reason. Identify any reversal, later proceedings, or distinction between the government's position and the individual's position. Add a source-access date. Preserve older coding in version history.

Case selection must follow an explicit sampling rule for any inferential dataset. Keep the purposive historical atlas separate. Link related opinions by litigation family and shared operational system before splitting training, discovery, and validation samples.

Do not upload sealed material, private family records, personal contact information, birth dates, government identifiers, account numbers, medical records, or credentials. Use only the information necessary to identify and understand public legal decisions. Do not describe a challenged mechanism as a proved defect unless the source establishes it.

Proposed corrections should state their impact on tables and charts. Re-run the analysis and include null or inconvenient findings. Do not silently revise a registered hypothesis, exclusion rule, primary endpoint, or stopping rule after viewing evaluation outcomes; record deviations explicitly.

The initial dataset received a structured coding review, not independent blinded duplicate coding of every source. Future releases should publish coder disagreements and adjudication results. Agreement is reproducibility evidence, not proof that a label is true.
